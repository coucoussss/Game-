# Package A2: Vehicle physics and driving

## Package-specific instructions
- Per-tank historical data (gears and ratios, governed rpm, max forward/reverse speeds per gear, steering type: Pz IV clutch-brake / Tiger & Panther & KT regenerative with neutral steer, turret traverse drives and real traverse times (Tiger hydraulic depends on engine rpm; hand traverse when engine off), gun elevation/depression limits and rates) must live in data (models.js SPECS or a table), not code.
- Gearbox with automatic shifting and engine rpm exposed so the audio package can use it (t.rpm 0..1, t.gear).
- Oriented-box (OBB) collision for tanks vs tanks/obstacles with mass-based impulses (no per-frame stalling), ram damage scaled by relative kinetic energy, wrecks that are static but pushable by heavy tanks only slowly.
- Ground types: real road network effect (lower crr, higher speed), mud/sand/snow per map with adhesion and rolling resistance, snow not sticky.
- crit01 (two world scales): evaluate carefully; if unifying the scales is too risky, at least make every user-visible distance and speed consistent and document the choice in a French comment. Do not break ballistics (package A1 already merged).
- Terrain heightfield and water are package A4 (later): design the physics so that a ground height/normal function can be plugged in later (e.g. a groundAt(x,y) returning {z, nx, ny, type} that currently returns flat ground).
- Keep AI driving working with the new steering/gearbox (AI uses the same controls); do not rewrite the AI (package A6), only adapt what breaks.

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [vehi00] Phantom speed: a tank blocked by an obstacle or the map edge keeps accelerating
- kind/impact/effort: bug/4/S
- files: game.js
- where: updateTank l.1071-1088 (t.speed integrates toward target, then collideObstacles + map clamp move only t.x/t.y); its consumers: collideObstacles q = mass*|t.speed| l.917-919, suspension bump l.1024-1025, dispersion l.1121, frame() Sfx.engine/Sfx.tracks l.2398-2402
- problem: Collision resolution corrects the position but never the velocity. I measured this with Playwright: a Pz IV pressed against the west map edge in Stalingrad had t.speed rise 6.4 -> 19.9 km/h in 3 s while its real velocity stayed 0 and its dispersion grew 0.0276 -> 0.0376. Consequences: (1) Walls break without a run-up. q uses the phantom speed, so a Pz IV pressing a wall reaches q = 25 t * 7.8 m/s = 195 > 140, and damage q*2.5 >= 350 hp deletes the wall in one step. Trees fall the same way. (2) Engine and track audio, tread animation and the sinusoidal hull bounce run at cruise level while the tank is stuck. (3) The gun gets the full moving-fire dispersion. (4) When the hull slides off a corner it leaps away at full speed. (5) Hitting a rock at 40 km/h causes no deceleration, so the suspension never sees the jolt.
- proposal: Store ox, oy and the pre-contact speed vPre before moving. After collideObstacles and the clamp, compute vAct = ((t.x-ox)*cos(angle) + (t.y-oy)*sin(angle))/dt. If |vAct| < |t.speed|, set t.speed = vAct: this cancels the velocity into the obstacle and keeps the tangential slide. Keep t.slip = target - vAct for effects (track-spin sound, dust, ruts). Use vPre, not t.speed, for the tree and wall momentum tests so obstacles only break on a real impact. Apply the same projection after tankCollisions. Verify by re-running the map-edge test (scratchpad phys/blocked.js): t.speed should stay ~0 and pressing a wall should no longer break it.

### [vehi01] WWII guns behave as if stabilized: shots ignore hull pitch and the sight shake is fake
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/M
- files: game.js
- where: fireShell l.1180-1186 (slope = tan(t.elev)/k3, z0 = T.S.gun.z); updateTank elevation l.1129-1136 (world-frame elevation, no roleOk(gunner) check); sightCam l.2586-2589 (jy/jp are pure sinusoids); render3D l.2706/2734 (the drawn gun = hull matrix susM * rotY(-elev))
- problem: suspension() and rock() already give a believable hull motion: about 1.7 deg nose dive on braking, several degrees on rams, rocking after each shot. The shell's launch angle, however, is world-referenced through t.elev. Moving fire is only penalized by a Gaussian multiplier (1 + 2.6 v/vmax + 1.6 w/wmax + 1.1 traverse), so every tank behaves as if it had a 2-axis stabilizer plus random noise. The rendered barrel bobs with the hull, but the shell does not follow it. The gunner-sight jitter is a fixed 0.005-0.007 rad sine that is unrelated to the hull's real motion. The gun also keeps laying in elevation when the gunner is dead.
- proposal: Make elevation hull-relative. t.elevRel is laid by the gunner at the handwheel rate (about 4 deg/s, 0 if no gunner) and clamped to per-tank limits. Shot pitch = elevRel + t.sus.p (sign as in susM). Hull roll cants the gun, adding a lateral error of about sus.r*tan(elevRel). Muzzle z0 += sin(sus.p)*gun length. In sightCam, drop jp/jy and drive the view from sus.p, sus.r (roll via the up vector) and the turret rate, so the reticle visibly wanders with the hull. Cut the movement dispersion term from 2.6 to about 0.6 (residual vibration only). This brings back the authentic short halt (Kurzhalt): stop, wait for the hull to settle, fire. With the current K=57 and C=4.5 (zeta 0.3, 1.2 Hz), settling takes about 3/(zeta*omega) = 1.3 s. AI: brake to a stop and wait 1-1.5 s before firing whenever it was moving. Verify: shell z spread at 500 m while driving reflects sus.p (+-1-3 deg); shots from a standstill are unchanged.

### [vehi02] Steering: every tank neutral-steers instantly at 52-132 deg/s and corners at 1.5-2.25 g with no speed loss
- kind/impact/effort: physics/5/M
- files: game.js
- where: updateTank l.1076-1081 (tr = turn*T.turn*engK*(1-0.3|v|/T.speed)*soil factor*grip, applied instantly); TYPES.turn l.69-105; suspension lat l.1024; Meta PARTS 'tracks' (grip) only scales yaw rate
- problem: Measured pivot rates at rest: Lynx 132 deg/s, Panther 88, Tiger 63 (Pz IV and StuG 86, King Tiger 52, Jagd 80 from data). Full rate is reached in one frame, with no yaw inertia. At top speed the yaw rate stays at 70-85%: I measured 2.25 g lateral acceleration on the Lynx (11.4 m/s at 111 deg/s) and 1.49 g on the Panther. Steel tracks cannot exceed mu_lat of about 0.5-0.7 g. Turning costs neither speed nor power. Real steering systems (approximate): the Luchs, Pz IV and StuG III used clutch-brake steering with NO neutral steer; they pivot about a braked track (omega = v_outer/B, centre radius B/2, B = W - tw: Pz IV about 2.48 m, Luchs 2.12 m). The Panther and Jagdpanther (MAN regenerative, one radius per gear, about 5 m in 1st to 80 m in 7th), the Tiger I (Henschel L 600 C, two radii per gear, about 3.4 m in 1st to 165 m in 8th) and the Tiger II (L 801) could neutral steer, but only in neutral gear and at roughly 15-30 deg/s.
- proposal: Add T.steer = 'clutch' | 'regen' plus the per-gear radii for regen tanks (these need the gearbox finding). Clutch tanks with turn input: brake the inner track and drive the outer one, so the hull walks around one track instead of spinning in place. Regen tanks: omega = v/R(gear); at v~0 in neutral, counter-rotating tracks give omega_n = k*rpm/rated, capped at about 25 deg/s (Panther), 20 (Tiger I), 15 (King Tiger). Add a first-order yaw lag tau = 0.25 s (Lynx) to 0.6 s (King Tiger), from Iz = m(L^2+W^2)/12 (Panther about 2.2e5 kg*m^2). Cap |omega| <= mu_lat*g/|v|; above that the tank skids outward (lateral slip velocity, dust, track squeal). Deduct steering power P_s = M*omega, with skid moment M = mu_t*m*g*L/4 and mu_t 0.5-0.7, from the drive power so tanks slow in turns and heavy tanks bog down pivoting in mud. Make the 'Grip Tracks' part multiply mu_lat. AI: accept non-zero turning radii for clutch tanks in path following. Verify (scratchpad phys/turn.js): a_lat <= 0.6 g, the Pz IV cannot spin in place, the Panther pivots at about 20 deg/s.

- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/M
- files: game.js, audio.js
- where: TYPES.tturn l.69-105; updateTank l.1110-1118 (ts = T.tturn * gunner alive * turret present); frame() Sfx.turret l.2403
- problem: Game traverse rates: Lynx 149 deg/s, Panther 80, Tiger 57, Pz IV 57, King Tiger 46, StuG and Jagd casemate guns 52 deg/s. A destroyed or burning engine does not change them. Commonly cited real values (approximate): Tiger I hydraulic, driven off the main engine, about 6 deg/s (360 deg in about 60 s) at about 2000 rpm and about 2 deg/s at idle, hand crank about 0.5-1 deg/s. Add an angular acceleration limit of about 30 deg/s^2 so tracking has inertia. A global TRAV_K arcade factor (x1.5-2) is a knob for CrazyGames playtests. Drive Sfx.turret from pump rpm. The AI already turns the hull for casemate tanks; extend that to slow turrets when the off-axis angle is over 30 deg. Verify: a Tiger reverses its aim 180 deg in about 30 s when idling and about 10 s when revved, and loses powered traverse when its engine is knocked out.

### [vehi05] Longitudinal dynamics: releasing the throttle is a 0.6 g emergency stop, there is no resistance force, and a speed floor bypasses terrain physics
- kind/impact/effort: physics/4/S
- files: game.js
- where: updateTank l.1059-1075 (braking = target===0 -> 0.6 G; aDrive = min(0.5G, P/(m v)) without crr; vmax = min(T.speed*engK, max(T.speed*0.5, vTerrain)))
- problem: Measured stops after simply releasing W: 10.6 m from 40 km/h (Lynx), 8.9 m from 37 km/h (Panther), 7.0 m from 33 km/h (Tiger). Real coasting deceleration is about crr*g plus engine drag, roughly 0.8-1.2 m/s^2, i.e. 50-80 m. Full track braking on dirt is about 0.4-0.5 g, and 0.2 g on snow. Acceleration never subtracts rolling resistance, so the speed rises linearly and then hits a hard cap. The floor T.speed*0.5 overrides physics on soft maps. On Bastogne (crr 0.09): Lynx physics 27.4 km/h but the floor gives 38.6; Pz IV 21.9 -> 24.4; StuG 22.8 -> 25.7. For the Lynx the Maybach engine part (+8%/level) therefore gives zero top-speed gain on that map at every level.
- proposal: Integrate forces: a = [sign(thr)*min(mu*g, eta*P*|thr|/(m*max(|v|,1.5))) - crr*g*sign(v) - g*sin(slope)]. Brakes apply only when the throttle opposes the motion or a brake key is held: mu_brake*g, with mu_brake about 0.45 on dirt and 0.2 on snow (from the soil table). Throttle 0 coasts with crr*g plus about 0.04 g engine drag. Delete the 0.5*T.speed floor. Keep T.speed only as the governed road top speed. Verify with drive_all.js: coasting from 40 km/h takes 50-80 m, braking 10-13 m, and engine levels change top speed on every map.

### [vehi07] Tank-tank contact: the impulse fires every frame, so pushing stalls at ~1 km/h; side hits never roll; no lateral friction, yaw or ram damage
- kind/impact/effort: physics/4/M
- files: game.js
- where: tankCollisions l.931-964 (vrel uses each tank's own pre-collision velocity t.vx from l.1088; J = 1.1*vrel*mu applied every step while overlapping; momentum only along each heading; rock(...,dirY=0) l.951-952)
- problem: Measured: a King Tiger (68 t) at full throttle into the side of a stationary Lynx (12 t) moved it only 1.0 m in 4 s, and the King Tiger's speed collapsed to 0.26 m/s, just under the 0.3 m/s impulse threshold. Pushing any tank or wreck caps you at about 1 km/h whatever the masses. Physically, the King Tiger's tractive effort (about 0.5*68 t*g = 330 kN) far exceeds the Lynx's lateral sliding resistance (about 0.6*12 t*g = 70 kN), while a Lynx (about 59 kN) could never shove a King Tiger (about 400 kN). When the struck tank's heading is perpendicular to the contact normal, it receives nothing while the rammer loses J/ma, so momentum is destroyed. rock() always gets dirY = 0, so broadside rams never roll the hull. A 40 km/h ram does no damage beyond a 50% thrown-track chance.
- proposal: Two-phase contact. (1) Impact only on first contact (store the touching pairs) with approach speed > 0.3 m/s, using actual velocities. Impulse J = (1+e)*mu_red*vrel, e = 0.1, applied in 2D: the longitudinal part goes into speed, the lateral part into a new slip velocity t.vl that decays at mu_lat*g. The angular part (r_contact x J)/Iz goes into yaw, with Iz = m(L^2+W^2)/12. Call rock(t, J, dirLong, dirLat, h) with dirLat = -sinA*nx + cosA*ny. (2) Sustained push as a force balance: pusher traction min(mu*m_a*g, eta*P/v) against the pushed tank's resistance (mu_lat*m_b*g sideways, crr*m_b*g plus brakes lengthwise), giving one shared velocity. (3) Ram damage from E = 1/2*mu*v_rel^2: above about 50 kJ, 30% chance of suspension or track damage; above about 150 kJ, a bent barrel for the lighter tank (dispersion x3 until repaired) and 3-10 hp crew bruising. A 'RAM!' score popup suits CrazyGames. Verify with scratchpad phys/push.js: the King Tiger pushes the Lynx sideways at >= 1 m/s and the Lynx cannot move the King Tiger.

### [vehi09] Collision shape is a circle of radius (L+W)/4 for 5-7 m long hulls
- kind/impact/effort: physics/4/M
- files: game.js
- where: makeTank r l.576; collideObstacles l.908-930; tankCollisions l.931-964 (shells already use OBBs in tankCand l.1512)
- problem: King Tiger: r = 2.79 m against a half-length of 3.69 m and a half-width of 1.88 m. Head-on, its nose sinks 0.9 m into walls, buildings and rocks, while two tanks side by side keep a 0.9 m air gap. Lynx: r = 1.78 m against a 2.3 m half-length. Pivoting next to a wall never collides because a circle is rotation-invariant, so a 7.4 m hull can spin in a 5.6 m alley. Movement collisions therefore disagree with both the visuals and the OBB hit boxes.
- proposal: Use an oriented box (T.len x T.wid px) against obstacle AABBs, and OBB-OBB tests via SAT (4 axes), returning the minimum translation vector and the deepest contact vertex. If the overlap comes from this step's yaw (pivoting into a wall), undo the yaw increment (store prevAngle) instead of translating. Keep circles as a broad phase. Feed the contact point into the angular impulse of the contact finding. Optionally push the hull back when the muzzle (gunEnd) is inside an obstacle, so the gun cannot fire through walls point-blank. Verify: a King Tiger against a building shows a 0 +- 1 px gap at both nose and side, and cannot pivot in a gap narrower than its diagonal.

### [vehi13] Obstacle impacts have no dynamics: binary wall breaking, trees felled by momentum, buildings never breachable
- kind/impact/effort: physics/3/M
- files: game.js
- where: collideObstacles l.915-929; damageObstacle l.2059; knockTree l.2080; genMap walls 14 px (2 m) thick l.348, rocks hp Infinity l.353, buildings hp 700/900 l.336/343
