# Package A4: Terrain relief, water and line of sight

## Package-specific instructions
- Add a heightfield per map (smooth procedural ridges, valleys, hills, craters; per-level character: Kursk rolling steppe with balkas, Ardennes forested hills, Stalingrad mostly flat with rubble mounds, El Alamein dunes and escarpment, Bastogne gentle snowy hills). Gentle enough that tanks can drive (max ~25-30 deg slopes, impassable steeper), with grade resistance, tilt from terrain (pitch/roll from the normal), sliding on steep slopes.
- Everything uses the same ground function: tank z, pitch and roll, shell ground impact height (ballistics), line of sight between tanks (ridges block LOS: hull-down becomes real), bushes/trees/buildings placement, nav grid costs (slope), camera and sight.
- Rendering: tessellated ground mesh with normals in world3d.js (keep shading and shadows working), craters as real depressions where feasible (or at least visual relief), tracks/ruts painted following the terrain.
- Water: rivers or ponds on some maps, fording depth per tank (historical: Pz IV ~1.0 m, Panther 1.7 m, Tiger 1.5 m (early with snorkel 4 m), StuG 0.8 m...), slow movement in water, engine flooding if too deep, bridges/fords as choke points, ice on Bastogne ponds (heavy tanks can break it).
- Hull-down data for the AI (positions behind crests) as a helper function (the AI package A6 will use it).
- Performance: keep the frame time reasonable (measure before/after with a quick 3D playwright run).

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [vehi11] Flat world: no heightfield, so no slopes, climbing, hull-down positions or grade resistance
- kind/impact/effort: feature/5/L
- files: game.js, world3d.js
- where: genMap/buildGround l.297-437 (flat 2D canvas); world3d.js ground (a flat textured plane via uGround); losClear l.689; sightProbe l.2607; updateShells/hitGround l.1440-1556 (ground at z=0); suspension bump synthetic l.1025
- problem: The ground is a flat plane. 'Bumps' are sinusoids of tread phase and ground z is always 0. Hull-down fighting, the core of Tiger and Panther tactics, is impossible, and so are reverse slopes, crests that expose the thin belly and lower plate, climbing limits and pitch or roll on slopes. Movement physics, line of sight, spotting and ballistics all assume z_ground = 0.
- proposal: Add a per-map Float32 heightmap at 4 m (28 px) from seeded value noise plus authored features: Kursk gentle swells +-3 m and balka gullies; Ardennes rolling +-8 m; Stalingrad flat with rubble mounds; El Alamein dunes and an escarpment; Bastogne ridges. Add helpers hAt(x,y) (bilinear) and normalAt. Tanks: sample the 4 track corners and use the resulting pitch and roll as the suspension's rest position, with hull z = their mean. Add grade force m*g*sin(theta). Climb limit where tan(theta) > mu: Pz IV, StuG and Luchs about 30 deg; Panther, Tiger and King Tiger about 35 deg. Vertical step: Pz IV 0.6 m, Tiger 0.79, King Tiger 0.85, Panther 0.9. Trench: Pz IV 2.2 m, Tiger 2.3, King Tiger 2.5. Ray-march the heightfield in losClear, sightProbe and spotting. Shells hit when z < hAt. In world3d, displace a ground grid mesh. AI path cost bonus for hull-down cells (a crest between itself and the enemy). Ship in steps: (a) heights, pitch and grade; (b) LOS and ballistics; (c) AI.

### [visu05] The terrain is a flat plane at z=0: craters, ruts and snow tracks are painted colour with no relief
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/L
- files: chars/world3d.js, chars/game.js
- where: world3d.js buildScene ground l.467-475 (8x8 quads at z=0), FS ground branch l.134-143; game.js stampTracks l.2248-2263, stampCrater l.2265-2271, suspension() l.1021-1030 (bumps are sines of tread phase), hitGround l.1540, ray3/aimRay3 (z=0 plane)
- problem: The ground is 128 flat triangles. An HE crater is a dark radial gradient of 18 px (2.6 m) and an AP hit is 7 px. Tracks are rectangles with alpha 0.16–0.22 every 9 px, with no rut or grouser pattern, and look the same in snow and mud. Tanks pitch and roll from procedural noise, not from the ground. The desert has no dunes and the steppe no undulation, so long-range shooting has no hull-down cover and no sense of relief. The whole simulation assumes ground at z = 0.
- proposal: Stage A, visual only (M). Add a height canvas hCv at half resolution (1300x850, 0.28 m texels, 8-bit, 128 = ground level). stampCrater writes a bowl −depth·(1−(d/r)²) plus a rim +0.25·depth between r and 1.3r. stampTracks writes ruts as wide as the track (S.tw), 4–8 cm deep (15 cm in snow), with grouser stripes every 0.15 m. Upload it as a LUMINANCE texture using the same dirty tiles as the ground. In the FS ground branch: `float c=texture2D(uH,uv).r, hx=texture2D(uH,uv+vec2(uHt.x,0)).r, hy=texture2D(uH,uv+vec2(0,uHt.y)).r; N = normalize(N + vec3(-(hx-c)*uHk, (hy-c)*uHk, 0.0));` (note the sign: uv.y = −y). Snow ruts then get shaded blue-grey walls and craters get lit rims. Optionally displace a 160x160 m grid of 2 m quads around the camera with the same texture in the VS (check MAX_VERTEX_TEXTURE_IMAGE_UNITS > 0, otherwise keep normals only). Stage B, gameplay (L). Use one low-frequency analytic heightfield H(x,y) in both JS and GLSL: 3 octaves of value noise at 0.6/1.5/3 m amplitude and 20/60/150 m wavelength, dunes as ridged noise in the desert, flattened at the spawns and along roads. Tank z, pitch and roll come from H at the 4 track corners (replacing the bump term). Shells hit the ground when s.z <= H(x,y). LOS samples H every 5 m, which enables hull-down. Build a 2 m ground grid with analytic normals. Check by firing HE at a fixed point with ?debug fireAt and comparing grazing-sun screenshots; for Stage B, add a JS-vs-GLSL height assertion overlay in ?debug.

### [vehi17] No water, fords, bridges or ice: fording depth, a major historical mobility trait, is absent
- kind/impact/effort: feature/3/L
- files: game.js, world3d.js
- where: LEVELS l.147-183 and genMap l.297-381 (no water features); updateTank (no water drag or flooding)
- problem: None of the 5 maps has a river, ford, bridge or frozen pond. Approximate fording depths: early Tiger I 1.2 m (about 4 m with its deep-wading kit), Panther about 1.7 m, King Tiger about 1.6 m, Jagdpanther about 1.55 m, Luchs about 1.4 m, Pz IV 1.0 m, StuG 0.8 m. Bridge weight limits, which kept Tigers off most bridges, are absent as well.
- proposal: Add a 'water' soil type with a depth grid (or derive it from the heightfield). Drag 1/2*rho*Cd*A*v^2 with rho = 1000, Cd about 1, A = W*depth, plus crr 0.15. Depth above the fording limit floods the engine (engine hp to 0 after 3 s, crew bails), and the submerged lower hull is shielded (AP shells lose velocity within about 2-3 m of water). Kursk: a Psel-like river with two fords and a 24 t wooden bridge that collapses under a Tiger or Panther. Bastogne: frozen ponds whose ice breaks under tanks above about 30 t. These would be memorable emergent moments for CrazyGames players.

### [ai08] No cover or hull-down: flat terrain, the AI holds wherever it first sees the enemy, and LOS heights are inconsistent
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/L
- files: chars/game.js, chars/world3d.js
- where: genMap l.297-381, obsH l.684-688, losClear l.689-706, aiThink hold branch l.802, aimPoint l.1168, inBush l.678
- problem: The ground is flat (no heightfield in game.js or world3d.js), so there are no ridges to go hull-down behind. The AI never seeks cover either: it holds (dest=null) wherever it first gets line of sight in its range band, usually in the open. Bushes are only consulted in updateSpotting and the HUD. Height handling is inconsistent. losClear ignores walls completely for vision but treats rocks and trees as infinitely tall, while shells use obsH heights, so a 1.9 m rock blocks spotting even though a turret sees over it. The AI also always aims at e.T.S.ht - 0.25: for a Lynx (ht 1.45) that is 1.2 m. So a Lynx behind a 1.3 m wall counts as in LOS for the AI, yet every shell hits the wall.
- proposal: (1) In genMap, add 'berm' obstacles: earth banks 1.7-2.1 m high, 120-260 x 12-16 px, hp Infinity, laid in lines facing the spawns on Kursk, El Alamein and Bastogne, with obsH('berm') = 1.9. (2) After obstacles are placed, precompute coverPts: along every wall, berm, rock and building edge, sample every 40 px at an offset of t.r+6 on both sides, storing {x,y,nx,ny,h,kind}; bushes add {kind:'bush'}. (3) In the hold branch, pick the coverPt within 180 px that has a blocker between it and the threat (segAABB), keeps turret LOS (blocker height below the shooter's gun z) and stays inside the range band, and set dest to it. Heavies and TDs reverse 25 px behind full cover while reloadT > 2 s, then come back up. (4) Make losClear height-aware: the sight line runs from the shooter's totalH+0.2 to the target's totalH, and an obstacle blocks only if obsH exceeds the line's height at that point. When the hull is masked, aimPoint uses the turret centre (ht + turret.h/2). Verify with a ?debug scene: an AI facing a target behind a berm aims at and hits the turret, and an AI holding near a berm ends up behind it.
