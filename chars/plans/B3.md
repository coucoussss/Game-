# Package B3: Game feel, HUD, onboarding, controls

## Package-specific instructions
- First-time user experience: CrazyGames players decide in 30 seconds. First launch: a clear 'Play' path straight into a guided first battle (interactive tutorial overlay: move, aim, fire, switch shell, use the sight, each step validated by the player's action, skippable, never shown again once done; option to replay it). A compact controls card on first battles.
- Hit feedback: reticle hit marker (penetration / ricochet / no-pen / critical, different shapes+colors+sounds), damage summary floating text, crew-kill notifications; ribbons and kill streaks in battle (Ammo rack!, Sniper 800 m, Double kill, Last stand...), queued messages that never overwrite each other.
- Damage direction indicator when hit, 'killed by' recap with the enemy tank, shell and hit location.
- 'Spotted!' warning (commander sense), short radio chatter text lines from allies (English).
- X-ray impact camera: never cover the play area for long on small screens; make it a skippable picture-in-picture that only triggers on notable hits, and let the player disable slow motion.
- Camera shake: frame-rate independent smoothed noise with rotation, recoil kick visible in the sight.
- Options: mouse sensitivity, invert Y, key rebinding (stored in the profile opts), assist level preset (crit02: 'Assisted' default = auto-ranging + penetration indicator; 'Realistic' = manual ranging with sight stadia and no verdict), colour-blind safe palette, reduce motion; options reachable from the pause screen.
- Gamepad support (Gamepad API: sticks, triggers fire/MG, bumpers shells, buttons sight/camera/pause) with on-screen prompts.
- HUD scales with the viewport (800x450 to 1920x1080 must be readable, nothing overlapping), results screen fits at 800x450.
- Garage/menus badges for new unlocks (meta12).
- Touch controls are package B4 (next round): structure input handling so that a touch layer can feed the same control state (e.g. a central `input` object with throttle/steer/aim/fire).

### [feel14] Camera shake is per-frame white noise, absent in the sight, and the firing kick is imperceptible
- kind/impact/effort: polish/3/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: fire shake l.1209 (5), hit shake l.1874/1884 (12/6), boom l.2245 (up to 22), updateCam3 l.2679-2681 (eye += random*shake*0.025, decay), sightCam l.2563 (shake neither applied nor decayed), 2D render l.2864
- problem: In the 3D view, firing your own 88 mm moves the camera eye randomly by ±0.06 m (5×0.025/2), invisible from 46 m away. Shake is new random noise every frame, so it feels like jitter that depends on frame rate rather than an impact. In the gunner sight, shake is never applied, and because the decay `shake *= …` sits after the sight early return, a nearby explosion while scoped leaves a stale shake that plays when you exit the sight. There is no camera pitch kick or FOV punch when firing.
- proposal: Use a trauma model: trauma ∈ [0,1], with addTrauma(k) at fire (0.18 + caliber/600), penetration taken (0.5), non-pen taken (0.25) and boom (k*(1 - d/900)*0.6). It decays at 1.6/s in a single place, at the top of updateCam3, before the sight branch. Offset = trauma² × smooth noise, sum of sines: yaw = 0.012*(sin(37t)+0.5 sin(71t+1)), pitch = 0.016*(sin(43t+2)+0.5 sin(89t)), roll 0.01. Apply it as a rotation of the look target in third person and add it to yaw/pitch in sightCam. Firing kick in third person: cam3.kick = 0.03*(caliber/75) rad of pitch-up and fov += 0.04, both springing back with a 0.25 s time constant. Add an option slider 'Camera shake 0-100%' (P.opts.shake) and respect reduced motion.
