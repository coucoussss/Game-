# Package B6: Retention, progression and economy

## Package-specific instructions
- Design for CrazyGames free-to-play retention with exact numbers: first session must unlock something within ~5 minutes; a new tank every ~3-5 battles early, then slower; result screen with next-goal progress bars (next tank, next rank, mission progress, mastery) and claim buttons; difficulty selection (Recruit / Veteran / Ace) with reward multipliers; 3-star sector ratings with first-clear rewards; persistent medals/achievements collection (30+ achievements with credit/XP rewards, shown in a new tab or in Missions); missions fixes (claim anywhere incl. result screen, unclaimed rewards kept, larger pool of 25+, weekly missions); per-tank mastery levels and crew XP with perks (e.g. Sixth sense, Quick reload, Smooth ride, Repair) chosen by the player; a free 'War Bond' season track of 30 tiers resetting monthly; daily supply crate; login streak that does not hard-reset (decays) and continues past day 7; records per mode and per sector with a local leaderboard of personal bests; Continue / Quick battle button remembering last tank, mode, sector, difficulty; ×2 booster only consumed on battles that pay at least a threshold; friendly fire pays nothing and teamkills cost credits; part prices scale with tank tier; duel and wave payouts proportional to the fight size; normalized rewarded ad payouts with per-day caps on bonus spins; near-miss hook on loss screen ('2 more hits on the King Tiger! Retry'); dead player spectates allies and can leave without losing earned rewards (crit06); historical variant upgrades as flavour where cheap (crit08).
- Difficulty must also feed the AI skill hooks (the AI package A6 comes later: expose a clean Meta.P.opts.difficulty and a skill multiplier helper).
- Everything persisted in the Meta profile (cloud-saved), with version migration (package B1 introduced versioning: extend it).
- Keep the stylish shop look (rarity cards etc.) for every new UI.
