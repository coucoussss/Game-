# Package B7: Allied factions and historical battlefields

## Package-specific instructions
- Add the Allies as a full opposing faction with historically accurate tanks, each with a parametric 3D model (models.js SPECS + builder: sloped T-34 hull, cast turrets, Sherman VVSS/HVSS bogies, etc.), crew layout and modules for the x-ray, armour plates (thickness/angle/cast vs rolled quality), guns and shells in ballistics.js with historical velocities/penetrations: USSR: T-34/76 (1943), T-34-85, KV-1S, IS-2 (122 mm D-25T, separate loading, slow reload), SU-85 or SU-100; USA/UK: M4A1 Sherman (75 mm M3), M4A3(76)W HVSS 'Easy Eight', Sherman Firefly (17-pdr with APDS), M10 Wolverine (3-inch M7, open top), M26 Pershing optional. Wet ammo stowage for late Shermans (lower ammo fire chance), petrol vs diesel fuel fires.
- Factions: the player's tank faction decides the sides: the player's allies share the faction, enemies come from the other side. Garage grouped by nation (Germany / USSR / USA & UK) with flags drawn in CSS or canvas (no external images), all purchasable with prices and rank gates (balanced against the German line), skins per nation (Soviet 4BO green with winter whitewash, US olive drab, British SCC 15...; same rarity system).
- Historical rosters per battlefield and year: Kursk 1943 (T-34/76, KV-1S, SU-122/SU-152 optional vs Tiger, Panther, Pz IV, StuG), Stalingrad 1942 (T-34/76, KV-1), El Alamein 1942 (M4A1 Sherman in British service, Grant optional, Crusader optional vs Pz IV), Ardennes and Bastogne 1944 (Sherman 75/76, Firefly, M10, Pershing (early 1945) vs Panther, King Tiger, Jagd, Pz IV, StuG). Rename/adjust battlefields if needed for historical consistency (crit00 notes anachronisms).
- Matchmaking by battle rating (crit03): each tank gets a battle rating; enemy compositions and rewards scale with the player's tank BR.
- Everything else (meta, AI, physics, damage) must work for the new tanks with no special cases beyond data.

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [crit00] Both teams drive the same 7 German tanks, and the battlefields are anachronistic
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/L
- files: game.js, models.js, ballistics.js, meta.js, index.html
- where: game.js TYPES l.67-110; TEAM l.141-144 (the enemy differs only by camo and a red accent); LEVELS waves l.146-183; spawnWave allyTypes/enemyTypes l.619-620; survivalEnemies pool l.658; models.js SPECS (7 German vehicles only; light = 'Pz.Kpfw. II Luchs' with a 5 cm KwK 39); index.html l.309 tagline 'Tank Warfare · 1943 – 1945'
- problem: Both sides are built from the same TYPES table, so every battle is Panthers against Panthers. The audit's realism work (armour, guns, crews) is entirely German-vs-German, and nobody proposed historical opponents. The waves also contradict history and the game's own 1943-1945 tagline. Stalingrad (1942-43) waves contain the Panther (first combat July 1943), the Tiger I (never at Stalingrad), the King Tiger and the Jagdpanther (both 1944). El Alamein (Oct-Nov 1942) fields Jagdpanthers, Panthers and Tigers. The 5 cm Luchs never entered series service. With identical models on both sides, telling friend from foe depends only on camo colour and markers.
- proposal: Add an opposing roster through the same data path (SPECS + Ballistics.GUNS + TYPES). Suggested vehicles, with values from memory that need checking before hard-coding: Soviet T-34/76 (45 mm @60° glacis, F-34 BR-350A 6.3 kg 662 m/s), T-34-85 (BR-365 9.2 kg 792 m/s), KV-1S, SU-85/SU-100, IS-2 (122 mm D-25T); British Crusader III (6-pdr), M3 Grant, Sherman Firefly (17-pdr APCBC 7.7 kg ~880 m/s); US M4A3(76)W (M62 7 kg 792 m/s), M10/M36. Add `nation` and `service:[from,to]` to TYPES, and `date` and `theatre` to LEVELS. Add a load-time check plus a node test that rejects any wave tank whose service window excludes the level date. Re-roster the waves: Stalingrad = Pz III/IV/StuG vs T-34/KV; El Alamein = Pz III/IV vs Crusader/Grant/Sherman (or move the Tiger to Tunisia 1943); Kursk = Tiger/Panther/Pz IV/StuG vs T-34/KV/SU-152; Ardennes/Bastogne = Panther/KT/Jagdpanther vs Sherman 76/M36/Firefly. Draw national insignia and turret numbers as decals so friend or foe reads at a glance. Later, offer a playable Allied side as a second campaign. Verify: node test over LEVELS, ?debug __fa.tanks lists both nations, and the bench prints penetration tables for the new guns.

### [crit03] No battle-rating matchmaking: enemies and rewards ignore the player's tank
- kind/impact/effort: gameplay/5/M
- files: game.js, meta.js
- where: spawnWave l.612-652 (enemyTypes = lv.waves[waveIdx]; allyTypes depend only on levelIdx); survivalEnemies l.656-667; meta.js battleEnd l.281-291 (no tank factor); TANKS l.79-82
- problem: I measured this in node with Ballistics.effective and penAt at 300 m (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Game-/ac915cfe-786c-573a-8bb1-e31901f2c033/scratchpad/critic/br.js). The King Tiger glacis (150 mm @50°) is worth 248-313 mm effective. The guns in Kursk waves 1-2 penetrate 97-102 mm (Lynx) and 138-169 mm (Pz IV/StuG). A rank-10 player who takes the King Tiger to Kursk is therefore frontally invulnerable, yet earns the same 150 ✪ per kill and 400 ✪ per win as a Lynx at Bastogne facing three King Tigers. The best loop is farming the easiest sector with the best tank, so progression erases its own challenge. The audit flagged flat rewards and the missing difficulty setting, but not tier matchmaking.
- proposal: Add T.br per tank: light 1.0, pz4/stug 2.0, medium 3.0, destroyer/heavy 3.3, kingtiger 4.0, plus the Allied tanks. In spawnWave, fill each slot with the tank from the level's historical pool whose br is nearest playerBR + [-0.7, -0.3, 0, +0.3, +0.7][slot]; do the same for allies at ±0.3. Weight the Survival pool to a br window. Rewards: kill credits = 150 × clamp(enemyBR/playerBR, 0.5, 2), and win bonus × (0.8 + 0.2·playerBR). Show the BR on tank cards and on the result screen. Verify: ?debug pick('kingtiger') at Kursk spawns Tigers and King Tigers; across 10 ?auto runs per tank, the win rate stays within 45-70%.
