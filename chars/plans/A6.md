# Package A6: AI and battle tactics

## Package-specific instructions
- Use terrain hull-down helpers from package A4, the new vehicle physics (A2), damage model (A3), and the difficulty/skill helper from B6.
- Squad commands for the player (ai03): keys and touch/gamepad bindings consistent with the central input layer (B3/B4): Follow me / Hold position / Attack my target / Move here (point under the reticle), with radio chatter acknowledgements.
- Wave formations and varied enemy compositions (ai15), class roles (scouts scout, TDs ambush from cover, heavies lead), flanking, angling armour toward the threat, retreat when crippled, focus fire, last-seen memory and gradual spotting, human-like gunnery (range estimation error and bracketing by skill), realistic reactions (no shell dodging).
- Fix every listed bug (wreck pathing, survival omniscience, stuck detector during intro, allied friendly fire...).
- Keep battles paced: first contact within ~30-45 s, battles 2-5 min.

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [ai00] Wrecks block AI line of fire but not AI pathing: tanks ram wrecks for up to a minute
- kind/impact/effort: bug/5/M
- files: chars/game.js
- where: buildGrid/markGrid l.441-453, findPath l.498-547, wreckClear l.731-740 (used in aiThink l.775), stuck block l.877-884, destroyTank l.2100, tankCollisions l.931
- problem: destroyTank leaves the wreck as a collision body (tankCollisions pushes by mass) and wreckClear makes it block ai.los, but the nav grid only stamps obstacles, so findPath routes straight through wrecks. I hooked ai.unstickT in Stalingrad wave 2 (?auto, speed 10) and recorded 47 unstick triggers in 100 s, almost all with a 0 px gap to a wreck. Ally 4 (Pz IV) rammed the same wreck at about (955,980) every 3 s for 35 s. Ally 2 (Tiger) did the same at about (1270,740) for 55 s, and Enemy 5 (King Tiger) at about (1568,890) for 40 s; none ever reached its first waypoint (pi stayed 0). In another probe, a Panther and a King Tiger 200 px apart with a wreck between them both had los=0 and pushed toward each other without firing. City and desert waves lasted 116-143 s against 35-55 s on open maps. Survival makes it worse because wrecks are never removed.
- proposal: (a) In destroyTank, add the wreck to a dynamic nav layer `wgrid` (Uint8Array GC*GR). Mark the cells within w.r+12 px as 1 (blocked), or as 2 (cost x2.5) when the wreck is less than half the mass of a heavy tank, which can push it aside. findPath then treats grid===1 || wgrid===1 as blocked. Re-stamp a wreck when it has moved more than 8 px; check this every 0.5 s for dead tanks. (b) In aiThink, when losClear is true but wreckClear is false, test the perpendicular offsets p± = t ± n·(T.wid*2 + w.r) and take the first one where wreckClear(p, best) && gridLineFree(t, p): dest = p instead of charging the target. (c) Unstick memory: after 2 unsticks within 25 px in 6 s, block the cells 30 px ahead of the hull in wgrid for 10 s and set ai.pathT=0. Verify by re-running the unstick hook on ?level=3&wave=2&auto: unsticks next to a wreck should drop below 5 per battle and median wave duration should fall.

### [ai01] AI pre-aims hidden targets through cover and fires instantly when they reappear
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: aiThink aim block l.886-904 (aim computed whenever ai.target exists), reaction reset only on target change l.779-780, hitTank l.1759
- problem: The aim is the target's exact current position (plus lead) whenever ai.target is set, even with ai.los false; the target is only known through the team-shared spotted set. reactT is re-armed only when the target changes. Measured in Stalingrad wave 1 over 90 s: in 195 of 206 samples where an AI had a target but no line of sight, its gun already pointed within 4.6 degrees of the hidden target. When the player pulls out from behind a building, the AI fires as soon as its reload ends, with no reaction delay. This kills peek-and-shoot, shoot-and-scoot and hull-down play, and turrets visibly track the player through walls. Related: when an AI with no target is hit, hitTank (l.1759) gives it the attacker's exact x/y.
- proposal: Give each AI its own memory, ai.mem = {x,y,vx,vy,t}, updated only while it has its own line of sight (losClear with vision=true && wreckClear). Without line of sight, aim at mem extrapolated by at most 1 s and let it drift slowly. If the target is known only through team spotting, aim along the reported bearing with about 6 degrees of error. Track ai.losPrev; when line of sight comes back (false to true), set ai.reactT = ai.react*rnd(0.5,1.0) and re-roll ai.aimErr with a 1.5x first-shot factor. The turret then slews from where it was pointing, limited by tturn. On being hit, store the bearing to the attacker (from the shell velocity) with ±10 degrees of error instead of its exact position. Verify by re-running the pre-aim probe: the ratio of hidden targets already aimed at should fall below 40%, and the gap between LOS regain and the first shot should exceed 0.3 s at skill 0.5.

### [ai02] Target selection is nearest-first: no threat, penetrability or focus fire, and no hysteresis
- kind/impact/effort: gameplay/5/M
- files: chars/game.js, chars/ballistics.js, chars/models.js
- where: aiThink target scoring l.771-784, aiShell l.753-762
- problem: The score is d * (los ? 1 : 1.7) * (crewFrac < 0.5 ? 0.8 : 1), so the nearest enemy always wins. It ignores whether the shooter can penetrate the aspect it sees, who threatens it, and what its teammates are already shooting. I ran aiShell's own maths offline with ballistics.js (APHE at 200 m against the upper front plate +10 degrees). Ratios: Pz IV and StuG vs Panther 0.70, Lynx vs Panther 0.45, every tank except King Tiger and Jagd vs King Tiger 0.66 or less. The AI fires at those fronts anyway: in a Survival probe, an AI Pz IV shot the player's King Tiger frontally from about 230 m for about 22 s. With no focus fire, the enemies' fire is spread over whichever ally is closest to each of them. With no hysteresis, tanks switched target up to 10-11 times in a 1-2 minute battle (hooked setter). Each switch re-rolls reactT (0.3-1.4 s) and may force a shell change that sets reloadT to at least 60% of the reload.
- proposal: Add targetScore(t,e,d,los). (1) aspect = the plate facing the shooter: |bearing - e.angle| below 35 degrees is front, 35-145 is side, otherwise rear, using armor.hull.up/side/rear and the turret plates. (2) pen = max over shells of penAt(t,k,d)/need(aspect). (3) threat = penAt(e,'APHE',d)/needAspect(t) * (e's gun within 5 degrees of t ? 1.5 : 1) * (e.reloadT < 1 ? 1.2 : 1). (4) score = (pen >= 1 ? 1 : 0.3+0.4*pen) * (0.6+0.4*min(threat,2)) * (1+0.25*alliesOnTarget) * (e === ai.target ? 1.3 : 1) * (crewFrac(e) < 0.5 ? 1.2 : 1) * (los ? 1 : 0.6) / (d+150); take the maximum. Rebuild a per-team focus count map once per think. Only switch when the new score beats the current one by more than 1.3x, or the current target has been out of LOS for more than 1.5 s. Verify with AI-vs-AI campaign runs: target switches should be 3 or fewer per tank per battle, and the share of shots at impenetrable aspects (hook shells.push and recompute the plate) should drop.

### [ai06] No flanking: when the frontal shot is hopeless the AI still drives straight at the target
- kind/impact/effort: gameplay/5/M
- files: chars/game.js

- problem: dest is always the target's position (no LOS, or outside the range band), null (hold), or a straight 160 px back-off. ### [ai09] The 2-minute global reveal plus smoke-blind AI LOS make concealment and smoke pointless
- kind/impact/effort: gameplay/4/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: updateSpotting l.718, aiThink LOS l.775 (losClear vision=false), deploySmoke l.1431

### [ai11] Accuracy model: perfect range and velocity knowledge, deterministic under-lead, no bracketing
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/M
- files: chars/game.js
- where: makeAI aimStd/lead l.560, aiThink lead and aimErr l.890-903, aimPoint l.1162-1170, hitGround l.1540, hitObstacle l.2022
- problem: The AI's error is an angular offset gauss·aimStd·(0.6+d/700), re-rolled after each shot. aimPoint gives it the exact distance to the target, so its range estimate is perfect. Lead is deterministic: target velocity · flight time · ai.lead, with ai.lead = 0.3+0.7·skill. A skill-0.3 gunner always aims about half short of a moving target: around 45 px at 400 px against a full-speed Lynx, a guaranteed miss. A skill-0.95 gunner always hits it. The AI reads the target's instantaneous true velocity, so jinking does not fool it. It does not correct between shots (no bracketing), the first error after a target switch is computed from the old distance, and target angular speed and tracking time play no part.
- proposal: Track ai.trackT (time on the current target with line of sight) and ai.corr (accumulated correction). Lead: use v_obs, an exponential average of the target's velocity (tau 0.6 s), times (1 + gauss·σL) with σL = 0.35·(1-skill)/(1+trackT). Range: first shot uses D·(1 + gauss·0.12·(1-skill)). Angular error: σ = aimStd·(0.6+d/700)·(1+2·ω/0.1)·(trackT < 2 ? 1.4 : 1), where ω is the target's angular speed. Bracketing: when a shell from an AI lands (hitGround/hitObstacle/hitTank with s.by.ai), store the miss vector relative to the target and set ai.corr -= 0.7·miss. Reset on target switch. Verify with an AI-vs-AI test: hit rate on a stationary target at 300 m should rise from shot 1 to shot 3, and low-skill AIs should score some hits on moving targets.

### [ai12] Armor angling is random and ignores the threat; movement and retreat expose sides and rear
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/M
- files: chars/game.js, chars/ballistics.js
- where: makeAI angleOff l.565, aiThink hold heading l.866-874, path following l.851-865, retreat l.800/l.823
- problem: While holding, the hull turns to its target plus ai.angleOff, a random ±0.25-0.45 rad (14-26 degrees), which is zero for the Lynx and turretless tanks. The value ignores the tank's armor layout: Tigers should angle about 30 degrees, while for the Panther, with sloped 40 mm sides, angling mostly helps the enemy. It is also measured against the AI's current target rather than the most dangerous enemy. When moving, the hull follows the path and shows its side. The smoke retreat drives to the map edge by turning around, because its waypoints are more than 160 px away, which shows the rear.
- proposal: Add bestYaw(t, threat). For yaw ψ from 0 to 45 degrees in 5-degree steps: front incidence iF = acos(cos sF · cos ψ), side incidence iS = acos(cos sS · sin ψ); compute effective thickness for each with Ballistics.effective and the threat's likely shell, adding a bonus from ricochetChance. Keep the ψ that maximizes min(effF, effS)/threatPen, cached per (t.T.id, threat.T.id, distance bucket). Apply it against the threat with the highest threat score from the target-selection finding, not against the target. In path following, when the next waypoint is within 300 px and lies behind relative to the threat (dot < 0), reverse (thr=-1) while holding the hull at ±bestYaw. Verify: an offline table should give about 30 degrees for the Tiger and 10-15 degrees for the Panther, and a probe logging hull angle against the main threat should match it.

### [ai14] Spotting is instantaneous and binary, with no size, stillness or last-seen memory
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/M
- files: chars/game.js
- where: updateSpotting l.707-728, drawEnemyMarkers l.3269, drawMinimap l.3579
- problem: Every 0.2 s, any enemy within range with a clear line of sight is spotted by the whole team at once, and dropped at once when that stops. Range ignores target size (a 4.6 m Lynx vs a 7.4 m King Tiger) and stillness: a parked tank is as visible as a moving one, apart from a x1.2 dust bonus. Bushes only cap range at 210 px when the target itself is inside one. There is no 'last seen' ghost marker, so there is no partial information and no way to set up an ambush, despite the bush and hatch systems.
- proposal: Use a detection accumulator per team: det[team].get(e) += dt·rate, where rate = clamp(1.6·(1-d/range), 0, 1.6) x (L·W/(6.9·3.42))^0.5 x (moving ? 1.3 : still for 3 s or more ? 0.7 : 1) x (in a bush ? 0.35 : 1) x (revealT > 0 ? 3 : 1). The enemy is spotted once det reaches 1; det decays by 0.5/s while unseen. Keep a ghost marker at the last seen position for 6 s on the minimap and in drawEnemyMarkers, and feed ai.mem from the pre-aim finding. Verify with a probe: a stationary Lynx in a bush at 200 px should take more than 1.5 s to detect, and a tank that just fired should be detected at once.

### [ai16] With a thrown track, the AI keeps driving and ends up broadside
- kind/impact/effort: bug/3/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: aiThink path following l.851-865, updateTank broken-track branch l.1063-1072

### [ai18] Shell and aim-point choice: only the upper front plate is considered, so the AI fires APHE at the upper hull every time

### [crit10] Only smoke shells block vision: no muzzle-blast obscuration, wreck smoke or dust
