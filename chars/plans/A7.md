# Package A7: Objectives, combined arms and operations

## Package-specific instructions
- Mission variety per battlefield: assault (capture zones held by time), defense (hold a zone against waves), escort (convoy of trucks/halftracks to protect), destroy depots/AT positions, breakthrough. Objective HUD, minimap markers, radio lines, rewards.
- Combined arms: towed AT guns in emplacements (Pak 40, ZiS-3, 6-pdr/57 mm) with crews that can be killed by HE and MG, anti-tank mines on some routes (mine-roller absent: detonation breaks tracks), artillery barrages (enemy pre-registered barrages + a player 'call artillery' ability on a cooldown, marked on the minimap, realistic HE splash and suppression), recon aircraft or ground-attack planes optional if cheap (shadow + strafing run, AA by MGs).
- Operations (crit05): campaign sectors as multi-wave operations where losses persist (damaged allies carry over, supply points to repair/rearm between waves, choice between two routes or reinforcements).
- Integrate with meta (rewards, missions, achievements from B6), AI (A6), audio (B5) and touch (B4) without breaking them.

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [ai07] No objectives beyond 'destroy everything': capture, escort, hold, destroy depots, defend
- kind/impact/effort: feature/5/L
- files: chars/game.js, chars/index.html
- where: LEVELS l.144-180, spawnWave l.612-653, step win conditions l.2357-2375, showResult l.4088, aiThink no-target branch l.811-815
- problem: Every battle in every mode ends only when one side is wiped out (step l.2373-2375; Survival l.2357). The campaign is 5 levels x 3 waves of the same 5v5 line-up spawning in two columns at x=200 and x=WW-200 (slots ±300); a level differs only by terrain and tank types. With no objective, the code needs artificial pacing: the 'noContactT > 20' hint that hands an AI a random enemy's position (l.788-795) and the 120 s global reveal (l.718).
- proposal: Give each wave an `objective` (keep the W_ arrays as `types`). Kursk: {kind:'capture', x, y, r:130, rate:1/45} on the crossroads. Ardennes: {kind:'escort', path: road polyline, trucks:3}, with soft-skinned trucks that have hp and follow the road; enemy AI gets +2 target score on trucks. Stalingrad: {kind:'hold', zone, time:240, reinforce: 2 tanks every 50 s} on the factory. El Alamein: {kind:'destroy', depots: 3 buildings flagged as depots}. Bastogne: {kind:'defend', zone: the town, relief at 300 s}. Add updateObjective(dt) in step(): capture progress only while one team alone is inside the zone, plus a win/lose rule per kind. In aiThink, with no target, dest = the objective; defenders take cover points around the zone, attackers push into it when it is contested. HUD: progress ring and world marker, plus an 'Objective' line on the result screen. Once objectives draw the AI into contact, the 120 s reveal can be removed. Verify: autoplay runs per level should end through the objective in 2-4 minutes.

### [crit04] No combined arms: no AT guns, infantry, mines, artillery or aircraft
- kind/impact/effort: feature/4/L
- files: game.js, ballistics.js, models.js
- where: The only entity type is the tank (tanks[], makeTank l.569); updateSpotting l.718 (comment 'after 2 min, the artillery betrays positions', but no artillery exists); MG vs tanks only mgHitTank l.1322-1400; canister fireShell l.1188-1193; explode l.2038-2057; LEVELS l.146-183
- problem: Only tanks exist on the battlefield. Anti-tank guns, infantry AT teams (Panzerfaust, PTRD, bazooka, PIAT), minefields, artillery and air attack caused a large share of WWII tank losses, and they define Stalingrad, the Kursk PaK fronts and the Ardennes. As a result, canister, HE fragmentation, the SmK/SmKH machine-gun belts and the exposed-commander game have almost no legitimate targets. 'Artillery' exists only as the excuse for permanent omniscience after 2 minutes. No auditor proposed any of this.
- proposal: Stage 1 (M): static AT-gun emplacements (pak40, zis3, 6pdr, 57mm-M1) as a new entity reusing GUNS, with 4 crew modules, a 10 mm shield and 1.2 m height. genMap places them in bushes and ruins, they get a small-silhouette spotting factor, and HE fragments, MG fire and canister can kill them. Stage 2 (S-M): a commander barrage (key B, once per wave): 6 rounds of 10.5 cm HE after a 20 s delay with a 40 m CEP, through explode(). An enemy barrage on tanks that stay still for more than 60 s replaces the waveTime>120 reveal. Stage 3 (M): infantry squads with Panzerfaust or bazooka (HEAT 100-200 mm, 30-60 m range) on the city and forest maps, killed by MG, HE and canister. Stage 4 (S): mines at chokepoints that break tracks and hit hull floors (10-20 mm). Add missions such as 'Destroy 3 AT guns'. Verify with ?debug spawns: MG vs infantry, HE splash vs an AT-gun shield.

### [crit05] Campaign waves do not persist: no operation with losses, supply or between-wave choices
- kind/impact/effort: retention/4/M
- files: game.js, index.html
- where: spawnWave l.616 (tanks = [] then a fresh makeTank for every slot each wave); showResult/resultButtons l.4088-4155 (each wave is a separate battle); resupply l.669-675 (Survival only, automatic)
- problem: Each of a battlefield's 3 waves starts from scratch. Every tank, the player's included, respawns with full ammunition, a healthy crew and repaired modules. Allied losses, rounds spent and wounded crew never matter beyond about a minute, and there is no decision to make between waves. A persistent operation is both the realistic model (losses and supply carry over) and a strong push-your-luck loop built from systems that already exist. No auditor proposed it.
- proposal: Keep an operation state across waves: {survivors: [{type, module hp, crew hp, ammo}], supply}. The next wave spawns the survivors plus reinforcements from the level pool, with rookie crews at a 0.8 skill factor. Between waves, show a 'Field HQ' screen (reusing the #result box) where supply points earned from kills and penetrations buy items: rearm by shell type (for example +10 APCR), repair the engine, tracks or breech, replace a crewman, or buy an artillery strike. Use the same screen in Survival instead of the automatic resupply. Pay a bonus for finishing an operation with N tanks surviving. Verify: in ?debug, kill an ally in wave 1; wave 2 should spawn a reinforcement and keep the player's ammo count.
