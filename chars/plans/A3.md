# Package A3: Damage model, crew and modules

## Package-specific instructions
- Morale and bail-out, fire system (ignition by location and fuel, spread, extinguishers: German tanks had a manual extinguisher and some an automatic engine-bay system; burn time and crew damage), ammo detonation from actual rack contents and propellant type, wounded crew efficiency, complete crew swaps (substitute moves seat), module damage states (damaged / destroyed) with effects, varied death outcomes (knocked out, burning, catastrophic with turret blown off and staying on the ground as a physics object), wrecks as low cover by height, field repairs only when stopped and not burning, with a progress indicator.
- Weak spots and non-penetrating consequences (ball07, dama14): barrel and turret ring hits, gun jam, optics cracked, concussion/spall from heavy non-pen HE (ball05).
- Keep the hitcam/x-ray narrative lines (English) consistent with new outcomes. Give the player clear readable feedback through the existing HUD messages (package B3 already added hit markers/ribbons: reuse them).

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [dama01] Instant ammo detonation is a high-probability coin flip that ignores what is in the racks (empty racks explode too)
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/M
- files: chars/game.js, chars/ballistics.js
- where: applySim() ~l.1959-1963 and 1968-1978; Ballistics.simulate frag() l.218, HEAT jet l.277, core l.331; rayMods() l.174-190; ammoFrac() l.597
- problem: Any entry in sim.ignite detonates the tank at once with probability 0.45+0.4*ammoFrac: 85% with full racks, and still 45% with empty racks. rayMods() includes modules with hp<=0, so a rack that was already destroyed or emptied can still be 'ignited'. The rack contents are not modelled: t.ammo is one global pool, and the HE/APHE share (sympathetic detonation) and brass-cased propellant (which burns rather than detonates) play no role. My node Monte Carlo on Ballistics.simulate (scratchpad dmgaudit/mc.js) gives ignition on 63% of 88 mm APHE side penetrations of a Panther and 77% on a Tiger. At full ammo that is about 54-65% instant destruction per side penetration. Detonation dominates the outcome, so kills feel random and the crew never gets a cook-off delay.
- proposal: 1) In makeTank, give each ammo module m.rounds={AP,APHE,...}, spread from T.ammo in proportion to box volume (m.s[0]*m.s[1]*m.s[2]); turret-frame racks fill first as the ready rack. fireShell takes rounds from the ready rack first, then from the hull racks, and t.ammo stays the sum. 2) In simulate(), skip ammo modules with hp<=0 or no rounds (pass a predicate through ctx). Scale the ignition chance by fill = rounds/capacity. 3) In applySim, per ignited rack: HEfrac=(HE+0.35*APHE+0.1*SMOKE)/rounds. Instant detonation Pdet=clamp(0.08+0.55*HEfrac+(hot&&jet?0.15:0),0,0.85). Otherwise it is a propellant fire: start a 'propellant' fire (see the fire finding) and set t.cookT=rnd(4,30) s, giving the crew time to bail out before a delayed detonation via destroyTank(...,'brewed'). 4) A destroyed rack ruins 30-70% of its rounds (subtract them from t.ammo; log 'Ammo rack hit: N rounds lost'). To verify, extend mc.js: instant detonations should fall to about 10-25% of penetrations, with clear differences between HE-heavy and AP-heavy loadouts.

### [dama02] No morale or bail-out: crews fight to the last man, and 'knocked out' only means fewer than 2 crewmen alive
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/L
- files: chars/game.js
- where: checkKnock() l.1999; applySim() knocked l.1969; crewLogic() fire l.994-1005; aiThink() retreat l.823; destroyTank() l.2100
- problem: A tank stays in the fight until crewAlive<2 or the ammo detonates. A burning tank keeps its crew inside taking 11 hp/s, and an immobilised tank with a destroyed gun keeps waiting for repairs. There is no morale value, no abandoning and no crew figures. The only stress reaction is closing the hatch (underFire, calm) and an AI retreat with smoke when crewFrac<0.6. Historically, most penetrated tanks were abandoned after one or two penetrations. Call destroyTank(t, t.lastHitBy, 'abandoned'): no boom or crater, kill credited to the last hitter within 20 s, mission counter 'bail'. Spawn each surviving crewman as a small runner entity (x, y, hp, 1.4 m/s toward its own spawn, 2D dot or 3D mini crewMesh) that MG bullets can hit (mgHitTank-like check, rnd(60,130) damage). For the player, prompt 'Press B to bail out' when on fire or cookT>0. Bailing out ends the tank but keeps a 'crew saved' bonus in the results. Add morale to the result stats and missions ('Make 3 crews bail out').

### [dama03] Fire lasts a fixed 8 s, rarely kills, never spreads, has no extinguisher, and a second ignition is ignored
- kind/impact/effort: realism/5/M
- files: chars/game.js
- where: applySim() fire l.1954-1955, 1962-1966; crewLogic() l.994-1005; burnFx() l.1010-1017; fire light l.2803
- problem: Every fire, whether from fuel, propellant or engine, sets t.fireT=8. It deals 11 hp/s to a random crewman (about 88 hp spread over the whole crew in total) and 5 hp/s to the engine, with a constant cook-off chance of 0.012/s (about 10% per fire). Then it always goes out. `!(t.fireT>0)` drops new fuel or propellant ignitions while a fire burns, so the fire does not intensify or get extended. Damage does not depend on where the fire is. The engine can be 'repaired' while burning. Flames always come from the engine deck (burnFx uses rx=x-0.3*len), even for a sponson or Pz IV mid-hull fuel fire. There is no automatic engine-bay extinguisher (fitted on Tiger I, Panther and Tiger II) and no manual one.
- proposal: Replace fireT with t.fires=[{src:'fuel'|'engine'|'propellant', mod:m, I, t}]. Start values: fuel 0.35, engine 0.25, propellant 0.7. Growth dI/dt: fuel +0.06, engine +0.04, propellant +0.12, capped at 1, and -0.15 under an extinguisher. Crew damage per tick: for each living crewman, 18*I*dt*exp(-dist(seat, fire module)/1.2 m), so the man next to the fire burns first. When I>0.6, it spreads to modules within 1.2 m: a fuel tank ignites with probability 0.1*I*dt, and an ammo rack with rounds cooks off with probability 0.03*I*dt, which starts cookT. The automatic extinguisher (T.autoExt=1 on heavy, medium, kingtiger and destroyer) fires once when an engine-bay fire passes I=0.3 and puts it out with probability 0.75. The manual extinguisher (KeyX, 2 charges, 3 s during which the gunner cannot fire; the AI uses it at once) succeeds with probability 0.6 if I<0.5 and 0.2 otherwise, never on propellant. No repairs while a fire is burning. Emit flames from the fire module's world position (convert m.c with turret rotation), and for propellant also from the hatches as tall jets; smoke density scales with sum(I). The HUD shows the fire source and an 'Extinguisher (X) ×2' chip.

### [dama04] Wrecks stop shells and bullets at any height
- kind/impact/effort: bug/4/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: tankCand() l.1520 (dead branch); updateShells l.1464-1468; updateBullets l.1280-1284; hitTank() dead branch l.1750-1755; updateSpotting() l.707-728 vs wreckClear() l.731
- problem: For t.dead, tankCand returns {part:'hull', z:1} whenever the 2D segment crosses the hull footprint. Unlike live tanks, it never checks zRaw against hull height. A wreck therefore acts as an infinitely tall wall: shots aimed over a 1.9 m wreck at a turret behind it, rising ricochets and HE lobbed over it all stop on it. The rule also disagrees with itself elsewhere: spotting (losClear) ignores wrecks completely, while the AI's wreckClear treats them as full blockers. Hull-down behind a wreck hides nothing from view, yet the AI refuses to shoot past one.
- proposal: In tankCand, handle dead tanks with the live height logic. If z<=T.S.ht, return a hull hit. Else, if T.tb && !t.turretGone && z<=T.I.totalH, do the turret-box test with the frozen t.turret. Else return null. Keep hitTank's dead branch as is. For consistency, make wreckClear height-aware: block only if the target's aim height (e.T.S.ht-0.25) is below w.T.S.ht+0.2. In updateSpotting, treat a wreck crossing the line as concealment for targets lower than the wreck's totalH+0.3 (multiply range by 0.5 instead of blocking). To verify, place a wreck between two tanks and shoot at the turret height of the far one: the shell should pass over a turretless wreck.

### [dama07] Module damage is binary, and destroyed ammo racks and fuel tanks have no effect
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/M
- files: chars/game.js, chars/models.js
- where: modOk() l.595; canFire l.602; engK l.1056 ('<' threshold); crewLogic repair to 0.5*max0 l.990; drawTankPanel MODS l.3537-3551; models HP l.244
- problem: Only the engine has a partial state (hp<50% → 60% power). Damaged breech, transmission, fuel and ammo modules do nothing. Destroyed fuel tanks and ammo racks also change nothing: t.ammo and the ready rack are unchanged, there is no fuel leak, and the panel turns the chip red for no reason. Field repairs set hp to exactly max0*0.5, and the threshold is a strict '<', so a 'repaired' engine runs at 100% power and stops smoking.
- proposal: Use three states with hp/max0 thresholds: ok (≥0.6), damaged (0-0.6) and destroyed (0). Damaged breech: reload ×1.3 and a 6% misfire per shot (6 s clearing). Destroyed breech: no fire. Damaged transmission: vmax ×0.65 and turn ×0.7. Destroyed transmission: no drive. Engine: engK = 0.45 + 0.55*clamp(hp/max0/0.6, 0, 1), so a field repair (0.5) gives about 0.9 and smoke continues below 0.6. Fuel tank damaged or destroyed: t.leak += 1, which doubles fuel-fire ignition chances and leaves a 2D fuel stain; a destroyed tank on a petrol engine also has a 25% chance of starting a fuel fire after rnd(2,10) s. Destroyed ammo rack: see the ammo finding (rounds lost). Kingtiger turret bustle rack destroyed → t.ready = 0.

### [dama09] Field repairs are unconditional: done in seconds while moving, burning or under fire
- kind/impact/effort: gameplay/3/M
- files: chars/game.js
- where: crewLogic() REP l.983-993; track timer l.1053, hitTank() l.1804-1807, heExternal() l.2004-2007, collision l.959

### [dama10] Nearby HE explosions break tracks far too often, and blast crew damage ignores position and exposed commanders
- kind/impact/effort: physics/4/S
- files: chars/game.js
- where: explode() l.2038-2046 (always passes face 'side', z 0.5); heExternal() l.2002-2020
- problem: explode() calls heExternal(t, s, by, 'side', 0.5, k) for every tank within R=75 px (about 10.7 m at PX=7), so the test `face==='side' || z<tt` always passes. Damage = clamp((P-150)/4, 0, 120) per crewman, consistent with simulate.explode. For an exposed commander (t.hatch), roll fragment hits with P = clamp(1 - dm/15, 0, 0.9) and damage rnd(40, 120), then set hatch=false. Log 'Commander hit by shell fragments'.
