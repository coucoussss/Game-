# Package B5: Audio: positional sound, engines, music

## Package-specific instructions
- Stereo positional audio (StereoPannerNode or PannerNode) relative to the camera/turret direction, distance filtering (air absorption lowpass), and speed of sound delay for distant cannon shots (sound arrives after the flash), supersonic shell crack + whistle for near misses, echo/reverb depending on map (forest, ruins, desert open).
- Per-tank engine sound models from the vehicle data (cylinders/rpm from package A2: t.rpm, t.gear; Maybach HL120 vs HL230 V12 characters), gear shifts, track clatter by speed and ground, turret traverse motor (hydraulic vs electric vs hand crank), enemy tanks audible by distance.
- Interior perspective when in the gunner sight (muffled exterior, louder interior: breech, loader, shell case ejection clang, crew intercom blips), realistic reload sequence sounds.
- Synthesized dynamic music (no audio files needed): a menu theme (majestic, slow, WWII orchestral feel with brass pads/strings/snare), battle music layers whose intensity follows combat (calm, contact, intense, victory/defeat stingers). Respect music volume, mute settings, ads mute, CrazyGames muteAudio (package B1 added hooks: reuse them). Remove any dependency on music/menu.mp3 but keep supporting it if a file is later added.
- Keep CPU cost low (limit concurrent voices, reuse buffers).

## Audit findings to implement (verify each against the current code first: line numbers may have shifted since the audit; earlier packages may have changed things)

### [feel11] Mono audio with no direction or distance character; enemy tanks are silent
- kind/impact/effort: realism/4/L
- files: chars/audio.js, chars/game.js
- where: audio.js noise()/tone() l.51-72 (connected straight to master), play() l.74, engine()/tracks() l.168-184 (player only); game.js listenerDist l.2221 and every Sfx.* call
- problem: Every sound goes source → filter → gain → master. There is no StereoPannerNode, so you cannot hear which side a shot came from. Distance only reduces volume through att(), with no low-pass (a cannon at 1 km sounds like one at 50 m, only quieter), no speed-of-sound delay, no echo or reverb (no ConvolverNode), and silence beyond about 1.19 km. Only the player's own engine and tracks make noise; enemy and allied tanks moving behind cover are silent, so sound gives no tactical information.
- proposal: (1) Spatial voices: change Sfx calls to accept a position, e.g. Sfx.fire(pos(mx, my), big) with pos = {d, pan}. Compute pan as the dot product of (source - listener) normalised and the camera's right vector, taken from cam3.view (r = [v[0], v[4], v[8]], with y converted back to game coordinates by flipping its sign as to3 does). Each one-shot chain becomes … → gain → StereoPanner(pan) → BiquadFilter low-pass with fc = 300 + 16000*exp(-dm/350) → master, plus a send to a shared reverb bus.
(2) Delay: t0 = ac.currentTime + dm/343 for fire, explosion and penetrate when dm > 120 m, so a distant duel is seen first and heard later.
(3) Echo and reverb: one ConvolverNode with an impulse generated at init (exponentially decaying stereo noise, 1.6 s for plain and desert, 2.4 s with denser early reflections for forest and city). Send level grows with distance (wet = 0.15 + 0.5*min(1, dm/800)). For cannon shots beyond 300 m, add a slap-back: a second low-passed copy (600 Hz) at +0.4..0.9 s and -10 dB.
(4) Other tanks' engines: a pool of 3 persistent engine voices (oscillator + low-pass + gain + panner), assigned every 0.25 s to the 3 nearest moving non-player tanks within 500 m, whether visible or not, with gain = (0.03 + 0.08*|speed|/T.speed) * att(d).
(5) Raise the audible range for cannons: use 1 - d/4000 for 'fire' and 'explosion'.

### [feel12] One generic engine for every tank, no interior sound in the sight, reload is a UI beep
- kind/impact/effort: realism/3/M
- files: chars/audio.js, chars/game.js
- where: audio.js init() engine (saw 38 Hz + square 19 Hz) l.21-28, engine(v) l.168-176, reload() l.150, fire() l.86; game.js frame() l.2398, setSight l.2551, missionToast l.2272 (reuses Sfx.reload)
- problem: The Lynx, Panzer IV, Panther, Tiger and King Tiger all use the same 34-86 Hz sawtooth, so tank choice changes nothing in the sound. Inside the gunner sight, the outside world sounds exactly the same as in the external view. A completed reload plays two square beeps (900/700 Hz and 400/300 Hz), and the same sound is reused for mission toasts, wheel prizes and revive, so it carries no gameplay meaning. The player's own shot uses the same recipe as any other tank's.
- proposal: (1) Per-type engines: ENGINES = {light:{cyl:6, idle:800, max:3200, lp:700}, pz4:{cyl:12, idle:600, max:3000, lp:520}, stug:{same as pz4}, medium/heavy/destroyer/kingtiger:{cyl:12, idle:600, max:3000, lp:420, rumble:1.3}}. Firing frequency = rpm/60*cyl/2: a V12 gives 60 Hz at idle and 300 Hz at maximum, a 6-cylinder 40-160 Hz. Use rpm from the existing 7-gear model in engine(v). Sfx.setTank(id) is called in spawnWave. Add a second partial at 0.5f for the crank and a noise 'exhaust' band-pass at 3f.
(2) Interior: split the master into an exterior bus and a self bus. When sight.on, the exterior bus goes through a 1200 Hz low-pass at -4 dB; engine and tracks get +3 dB plus a 30 Hz rattle LFO on the gain. Ramp the change over 0.15 s with a periscope 'clunk' (tone 120→80 Hz plus noise at 900 Hz).
(3) Breech and loading: on fire, add a casing ring for the player only at +0.6 s (triangle 1100→1000 Hz, 0.4 s). When reloading ends, play the breech closing (noise band-pass 1.8 kHz for 40 ms plus tone 160→120 Hz for 0.15 s) instead of the beeps. Move the beeps to Sfx.notify for missions, wheel and revive.
(4) Own shot: add a 45 Hz sub-bass tone for 0.8 s and a stereo-wide reverb send when d < 60 px.

### [feel13] No music in battle; the menu music file is missing and errors on every click
- kind/impact/effort: polish/3/M
- files: chars/index.html, chars/game.js, chars/audio.js, chars/music/README.md
- where: index.html l.461 <audio id=menuMusic src=music/menu.mp3>; game.js playMusic/stopMusic l.4011-4017, start button stopMusic l.4261, window pointerdown → playMusic l.4377; dist zip contains only music/README.md
- problem: music/menu.mp3 is neither in the repo nor in dist/steel-front-crazygames.zip, so the menu is silent. Every pointerdown in the menu calls music.play() on the missing file; Playwright logged 'ERR_FILE_NOT_FOUND …/music/menu.mp3', and on CrazyGames this becomes a 404 per click. Battles have no music at all (stopMusic at Deploy, never restarted), so the long approach phase is quiet, and there is no tension build-up or victory/defeat stinger.
- proposal: Add a procedural Music module to audio.js on its own gain bus (P.opts.music). Layers in D minor at 84 bpm, chords i-VI-III-VII at 2 bars each:
- pad: 2 detuned saws through a 700 Hz low-pass, slow attack;
- ostinato: triangle at 73.4 Hz on eighth notes;
- snare: band-pass noise at 1.8 kHz in a march pattern;
- brass: saw stabs with a filter envelope.
Intensity I = smoothed (time constant 3 s) max of: spotted enemies/3; 0.8 if a shell has s.warned; 0.7 if player.underFire > 0; 0.6 if crewFrac < 0.6. Layer gains are smoothstep(0,0.2), (0.25,0.45), (0.5,0.7) and (0.75,0.9) of I. Stingers on WAVE WON/LOST and the ribbon cues. The menu uses pad + ostinato. Either delete the <audio> element or listen for its 'error' event once and set a flag that stops further play() calls. Duck the music by 6 dB during the hitcam and pause it during ads (hooks.adStart already exists).
