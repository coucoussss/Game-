Controls

WS / ZS / ↑↓ forward, reverse · AD / QD turn · X brakes
Mouse: aim in 3D, high or low on the target · click: lock the cursor
Left click / Space: fire · F machine gun · G bullet type
1–7 shells: AP, APHE, APCR, HEAT, HE, smoke, canister
Shift / V gunner's sight · wheel: magnification or zoom
U commander out of hatch · E smoke launcher · T fire extinguisher · C camera · L flare (night)
R crew out to repair a broken track (stopped) · B bail out of a burning tank
Orders to allies: 8 attack my target · 9 follow me · 0 hold position · N / middle click: move there (same key again: free engagement)
H X-ray impact camera (skip it, or change its mode) · M sound · ? controls card · Esc / P pause
Realistic aiming: in the sight, the wheel or PgUp PgDn set the range, Z changes the magnification
Every key can be changed in Options · a gamepad works too (sticks, triggers, bumpers)
Left thumb anywhere on the left: drive (virtual stick) · drag on the right: aim
FIRE: fire · MG (hold): machine gun · AMMO: next shell, or tap a shell in the panel
SIGHT: gunner's sight · ZOOM / pinch: magnification or camera distance
Top-left buttons: pause, camera, smoke, hatch, flare (night), repairs and orders to allies
A keyboard works at the same time: any key brings the keyboard hints back

Realism

Sloped plates: effective armor = thickness / cos(angle). Too shallow: ricochet.
3D ballistics: drop, drag, penetration from impact velocity.
No hit points: crew, engine, ammo, breech and fuel tanks.
Wounded crew work slower. Fires spread, racks cook off: crews bail out.
Armor keeps every scar and weakens around each impact.
Friendly fire hurts. Bushes hide you, but firing gives you away.
Muzzle blast dust, shell bursts and burning wrecks block the view: on sand your own shot hides the target for a moment.
